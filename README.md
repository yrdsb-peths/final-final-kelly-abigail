**What is your project about?**
  To begin, our game is about the character Winnie the Pooh (player) who needs to get to the end of the game (honey) in order to win. 
  Throughout the game, there are two consistent obstacles. 
  First, there are enemies (bees) where if Winnie collides with one he will lose one life. 
  The other is not falling through the ground tiles. 
  Everytime that Winnie jumps on an enemy (bee) he gains 10 points and the player should finish with at least 150 points (self goal).

**How does someone get started playing it?**
  First, you will be sent to the starting screen. 
  If you want to learn how to play the game, press the I key on your keyboard.
  If you want to begin the game immediately, press the space bar. If you are on the instruction screen, press space to start the game.
  Use the arrow keys to move: left, right, and up. Jumping on enemies will defeat them. Be careful not to let an enemy touch Winnie, or you will lose a life. 
  You have three lives in total. If you lose all your lives, press space to restart the game.

**Is there anything the teacher should know (e.g. cheat mode to get to the end screen)**
  The only way to win the game is through hard work: your blood, sweat, and tears.
